# Johnny Joestar outfit
`JohnnyOutfit` (in the core, Spin_Server). Applied while a character plays any Tusk Act (1-4), players and NPCs. Matched to the user's reference art.

![[johnny_outfit_preview.png]]
- **Cap:** a light-blue knit cap covered in pink stars, with a folded band, little frayed tips, and a gold horseshoe on the front (opening upward) with a black horse head inside.
- **Hair and neck:** wavy blond hair to his shoulders, and a lavender scarf.
- **Top:** light blue, with a black V across the chest (front and back) and a purple star patch on the right sleeve.
- **Arms:** sleeves pushed up to lavender cuffs at the elbow, bare forearms, black wristbands.
- **Legs:** light-blue trousers covered in pink stars, and dark shoes.
