# Tusk Act 4: Infinite Rotation (gold + deep blue)
Reached by using the previous Act's ultimate. Numbers are in `TuskConfig.Act4`.

**Stand:** Tall humanoid in deep blue with gold metal trim, glowing cyan eyes and two golden rings that always orbit it (built in code; send a reference image to refine it).

| Input | Move |
|---|---|
| M1 ×4 | INFINITE BARRAGE: heavy punch, golden strike, rapid triple-hit, golden area finisher |
| 1 | GOLDEN SPIN NAIL: big rotating projectile with three golden rings, golden impact blast |
| 2 | INFINITE ROTATION (hold): stage 1 small rings, stage 2 more circles, stage 3 golden-blue + distortion; bigger shot and blast per stage |
| 3 | GOLDEN PURSUIT: golden rush to the first enemy ahead, three rotational strikes, huge golden shockwave |
| G | INFINITE ROTATION: 10-step cinematic (camera push-in, darkening, Stand manifests, golden rings, staged build-up) then a giant golden projectile and a battlefield-wide shockwave. Final form. |
