# Tusk Act 3: Master of spatial movement
Reached by using the previous Act's ultimate. Numbers are in `TuskConfig.Act3`.

**Stand:** Slender, ghostly humanoid in pink and navy, with a swirling tail (built in code; send a reference image to refine it).

| Input | Move |
|---|---|
| M1 ×4 | WORMHOLE STRIKES (fast, portal uppercut finisher) |
| 1 | DIMENSIONAL STEP: validated 28-stud portal teleport with entrance and exit portals and afterimages |
| 2 | SPATIAL PURSUIT: four portals open around the target one by one; each nail re-aims at where the target is now |
| 3 | DIMENSIONAL BREAK: energy gathers, a large portal opens, a 34-stud dimensional shockwave tears forward |
| G | INFINITE DIMENSION: eight layered portals around the battlefield, dimensional collapse, then **evolves to [[Tusk Act 4]]** |
