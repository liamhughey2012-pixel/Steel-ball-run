# Blackmore: Catch the Rainbow
Pick him from the character dropdown (BLACKMORE). Numbers: `TuskConfig.Blackmore`.
Server moves: `TuskActs/Blackmore`. Visuals: `TuskActVFX/BlackmoreVFX`. Look: `BlackmoreOutfit`. Scripts: `Blackmore_Server` + `Blackmore_Client`.
Outfit matched to the reference art the user sent (2026-11-23).

![[blackmore_preview.png]]
*(stand-in render on a test avatar)*

**Look** (blocky, from the reference art): a dark navy hood round the head and neck with a gold ring on top and a long blond ponytail sweeping up and back; his white mask with the diagonal rainbow stripe (red, yellow, blue), eye slits and three black slits; a navy capelet over the shoulders and down to the elbows, covered in gold grommets (two rows down the front); a long navy coat split at the front; pale grey-blue sleeves and leggings with dark raindrop marks; grey studded boots.

**Umbrella** (dark navy-purple): always in his left hand, closed like a cane. During CATCH THE RAINBOW it opens above his head.

**Catch the Rainbow:** freezes raindrops in mid-air, then cuts with them, walks on them, and scatters into them.

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | RAINDROP COMBO | two blade sweeps, a kick, then a finisher that fans out hardened drops (launch) |
| 1 | RAINDROP BLADES | 7 drops freeze in a fan in front of him, shiver, and fire as a volley (3 each, max 3 per enemy). 6 s |
| 2 | RAINDROP STEP | dashes 28 studs along a stair of frozen drops, with i-frames. The drops hang there for 3 s, and anyone walking through the line is cut (2). 7 s |
| 3 | RAINDROP CAGE | a dome of frozen drops round the nearest enemy (16 studs): 5, slowed to 45% for 2.5 s, and every time they move the drops cut them (2). 11 s (3 s on a miss) |
| Jump in mid-air (while raining) | RAIN WALK | he steps on frozen raindrops and walks on the air; jump again to step higher (6 s, 8 steps, 3 s cooldown). See [[Weather]] |
| G | CATCH THE RAINBOW | a dark cloud, a downpour and a rainbow (18-stud radius, 5 s). The rain slices every enemy inside (1.5 every 0.5 s). The **first hit on him** makes him burst into raindrops and reform out of reach. At the end, every frozen drop fires inward (12, launches) |

- He's also a Stand-user NPC and a bounty-board target ("STAND: CATCH THE RAINBOW").

## Animations
- **Stance:** calm and upright, with the umbrella held like a cane.
- **Combo:** a right sweep, a left sweep, a front kick, then a palm wind-up into a final sweep.
- **Blades:** winds his arm back, then snaps it across.
- **Step:** a high stepping run along the drops.
- **Cage:** an open palm toward them, then a closing fist.
- **Storm:** both arms raised to the sky, then holds.
