# Tusk Act 2: Wormhole combat
Reached by using the previous Act's ultimate. Numbers are in `TuskConfig.Act2`.

**Stand:** rebuilt from the owner's reference images: pink mechanical body with a ribbed chest, a round head with a ribbed visor face and a blue star, two gold rods ending in purple drums, a pincer claw, and a big dotted pod with a yellow-and-blue star (built in code; send a reference image to refine it).

| Input | Move |
|---|---|
| M1 ×4 | DIMENSIONAL COMBO |
| 1 | WORMHOLE NAIL: the nail opens a wormhole where it lands; a second nail bursts out of an exit portal behind the nearest enemy |
| 2 | UNDERGROUND PURSUIT: burrow, travel under the ground, emerge behind the target with a launch |
| 3 | NAIL DIMENSION: three portals around the target fire converging nails, then a dimensional shockwave |
| G | DIMENSIONAL STORM: six portals rain three waves of nails, a giant blue burst, then **evolves to [[Tusk Act 3]]** |

Status: 🧪 code complete, not yet tested in Studio. See [[Test Checklist]].

## The Golden Ratio (replaces the portals)
Everywhere Act 2 used to open wormhole portals (M1 hits, the nail fingertip, the ring around the target, the finisher, the ultimate), it now opens a **Golden Rectangle**: nested golden-ratio rectangles with the golden spiral drawn through them, plus a spinning golden spiral, in gold. Nails fly out of them with gold-and-cyan trails.
Renamed: GOLDEN COMBO (M1), GOLDEN RATIO NAIL (1), UNDERGROUND PURSUIT (2), GOLDEN SPIRAL (3), GOLDEN RATIO STORM (ultimate). Tagline "The Golden Ratio".
