# Hot Pants: Cream Starter
Pick her from the character dropdown (HOT PANTS). Numbers: `TuskConfig.HotPants`.
Server moves: `TuskActs/HotPants`. Visuals: `TuskActVFX/HotPantsVFX`. Look: `HotPantsOutfit`. Scripts: `HotPants_Server` + `HotPants_Client`.
Outfit matched to the reference art the user sent (2026-11-23).

**Look** (blocky, from the reference art): a pink-purple bob with bangs; a black cap with a gold front plate, rivets, a gold visor and a gold spike on top; a thick cream scarf; a pink tunic dress with a shoulder capelet, gold four-petal flower clasps, gold leaf studs, a darker pink round panel and a black fur hem; a gold chain belt with square links, a round HP buckle and a holster; cream ribbed sleeves and leggings; tall flared pink boots with pointed cream toes.

**Cream Starter:** a pink spray can (gold label, white cap) that sprays flesh. She **always holds it in her right hand** (players and NPCs), upright in her fist with the nozzle pointing down her arm; the spray comes out of the nozzle and follows her arm as she sweeps.

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | SPRAY COMBO | jab, front kick, a spray burst in the face, roundhouse finisher |
| 1 | FLESH SPRAY | a cone of pink flesh mist for 0.6 s (15 studs): 4 ticks of 2, and everyone hit is **coated** in flesh lumps and slowed to 60% for 2.5 s. 7 s |
| 2 | FLESH SEAL | flesh sprayed over the nearest enemy's **mouth and nose** (11 studs): 4, then they **suffocate** for 3 s (2 every 0.5 s, slowed), "MMPH!", a pink haze on their screen. 12 s (3 s on a miss) |
| 3 | FLESH MEND | she sprays her own wounds shut: **heals 18** over 1.2 s ("+4.5" pop-ups) and clears seals / coatings on her. 16 s |
| G | FLESH STORM | a flesh cloud swells in front of her, hardens into a **cocoon** round everyone caught (5, held 1.6 s, crushed for 2 every 0.4 s), then the cocoons burst (12, launches) |

- Also a Stand-user NPC and a bounty-board target ("STAND: CREAM STARTER").

## Animations (2026-11-23)
- **Stance:** can held up by her shoulder, left hand on her hip.
- **Combo:** left jab (the right hand has the can), high kick, a can thrust that sprays in their face, jumping spin kick.
- **Flesh Spray:** shakes the can three times, then sweeps the spray left, right and back.
- **Flesh Seal:** a shake, a dash-lunge with the can, and she presses it into their face.
- **Flesh Mend:** sprays from her shoulder down her body and back up, then a relieved breath.
- **Flesh Storm:** shakes the can hard, raises it overhead, sweeps the cloud left and right, then a final thrust.
