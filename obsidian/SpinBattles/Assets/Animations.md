# Animations
All moves currently use **procedural poses** (Motor6D C0 offsets: no uploads needed, R15 and R6).
To use real animations: upload them to your account, then paste the ids into `TuskConfig.AnimationIds` (Idle, Walk, Run, Sneak, M1_1…M1_4, NailShot, NailBarrage, SpinningNailCharge/Release, Awakening, Burrow, Emerge). A filled id overrides the procedural pose for that move.
