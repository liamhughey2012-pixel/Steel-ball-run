# Vision
A premium anime Battlegrounds game set in the Steel Ball Run race: Spin mechanics, a four-Act [[Tusk]] progression, Steel Balls and Wrecking Balls, cinematic ultimates, and layered procedural VFX. Combat is server-authoritative.

- Act 1 = the beginning of Spin
- Act 2 = wormhole combat
- Act 3 = master of spatial movement
- Act 4 = overwhelming Infinite Rotation (gold + deep blue identity)
