Don't rename anything. The scripts find each other by these exact names.

Install steps

  1. Unzip SpinBattlegrounds.zip anywhere on your PC.
  2. Open your place in Roblox Studio and show the Explorer (View › Explorer).
  3. Right-click ReplicatedStorage › Insert from File… › choose install/SpinShared.rbxmx.
  4. Right-click ServerScriptService › Insert from File… › choose install/SpinServer.rbxmx.
  5. Open StarterPlayer, right-click StarterPlayerScripts › Insert from File… › choose install/SpinClient.rbxmx.
  6. Press Play. Three practice dummies appear in front of the spawn. Hold R, let go to throw.
  7. Optional sounds: upload the 5 files in assets/audio in Creator Hub › Asset Manager, then paste each id into SpinShared › SpinConfig › Sounds as "rbxassetid://123…".
  8. Optional, if you have your own combat system: open SpinServer › CombatAdapter and fill in Hooks so Spin uses your damage and blocking.
If you use Rojo instead: run rojo serve in the project folder and press Connect in the Rojo plugin. It builds the same tree.

Spin design

Hold to charge, release to throw. Every number lives in SpinConfig.

LevelChargeDamageStunCooldownLook
QUICK SPIN0 – 0.6 s70.2 s2.5 s1 pink-white rotation ring
POWER SPIN0.6 s +130.4 s4.5 s2 rings, ground dust
PERFECT SPINrelease at 1.35 – 1.57 s220.65 s7 s3 golden rings, speed lines, impact frame

Rules

  • Holding past the gold zone drops the throw back to POWER (overcharge). At 3 s it throws automatically.
  • The server times the charge itself and allows ±0.05 s for lag. The client's meter is only a display.
  • Counterplay: the thrower moves at half speed while charging, getting hit interrupts the charge, the ball is visible and can be dodged, blocking takes 35% damage with no stun, and 0.5 s of stun immunity prevents stun-locks.
  • Controls: PC R (aims at the mouse) · Controller RT · Mobile on-screen SPIN button (camera aim).

Known issues and limits

  • Not yet run in Roblox Studio. Every file passes a Lua syntax check, and the charge-level and Perfect-window maths was tested in a Lua interpreter.
  • Arm pose angles and ring sizes may need tuning after the first test (ProceduralPose.Poses, SpinConfig).
  • No lag compensation yet, so very high-ping targets can be hit slightly behind where they appear.
  • The default stun slows WalkSpeed. If your game has a sprint system, use CombatAdapter.Hooks.ApplyStun.
  • Sounds stay silent until their ids are pasted into SpinConfig.Sounds.

Ground rules

  1. Inspect the existing project before changing it.
  2. Keep working systems; never build a second combat framework.
  3. Small modular Luau files.
  4. The server decides damage, hits, cooldowns and teleports.
  5. Every effect cleans itself up and respects the quality setting.
  6. No fake buttons or unexplained placeholders.
  7. Original assets and sounds only.
  8. Ask before building any weapon or Stand model, so you can send a model or image first.
